# Fernhill Insurance Agency: Customer FAQ

Fictional agency used for the demo. Each entry has a fixed id. Customer replies may only state facts found here, and must cite the id.

## FAQ-01: What are your office hours?
Our office is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, and Saturday from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## FAQ-02: Where is your office and can I walk in?
Our office is at 214 Market Street, Fernhill. Walk-ins are welcome during office hours. For a new policy we recommend booking an appointment so a producer is free to help you.

## FAQ-03: How do I reach you?
Call us on (555) 555-0140 or reply to any email from us. We answer messages within one business day.

## FAQ-04: How long does a quote take?
Once we have all the information on our quote checklist, we usually send your quote within two business days.

## FAQ-05: Does a quote cost anything?
Quotes are free and there is no obligation to buy.

## FAQ-06: What types of insurance do you offer?
We offer auto, home, commercial and life insurance. We do not offer health insurance.

## FAQ-07: Which insurance companies do you work with?
We work with four carriers: Northwind Mutual, Granite Shore Casualty, Larkspur Life and Keystone Way Commercial. We compare them to find the best fit for you.

## FAQ-08: How can I pay my premium?
You can pay by bank transfer, debit card, credit card or check. We cannot accept cash at the office.

## FAQ-09: Can I pay monthly?
Yes. Most policies can be paid monthly, quarterly, every six months or once a year. Some carriers add a small installment fee for monthly payments.

## FAQ-10: What happens if I miss a payment?
Contact us as soon as possible. Each carrier sets its own grace period, which is shown on your billing notice. We cannot extend a carrier's grace period.

## FAQ-11: How do I add a driver to my auto policy?
Send us the driver's full name, date of birth, driver's license number and the date they were first licensed. The change takes effect once we confirm it to you in writing.

## FAQ-12: How do I add a vehicle to my auto policy?
Send us the vehicle's VIN, year, make and model, and the lender's name if it is financed. Coverage for the new vehicle starts once we confirm it to you in writing.

## FAQ-13: How do I get proof of insurance or new ID cards?
We email proof of insurance and ID cards within one business day of your request. You can also download them from your carrier's app.

## FAQ-14: How do I file a claim?
Report the claim to your carrier first, using the claims number on your ID card. Carrier claims lines are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Then let us know, and we will help you follow up.

## FAQ-15: Can you check the status of my claim?
Yes. Send us your claim number and we will check the status with the carrier for you.

## FAQ-16: Does my policy include roadside assistance?
Roadside assistance depends on your policy and carrier. Send us your policy number and we will check whether it is included.

## FAQ-17: What do you need to quote home insurance?
We need the property address, the year the home was built, the square footage, the age of the roof, the construction type and any claims in the last five years.

## FAQ-18: Does home insurance cover flood?
Standard home policies do not cover flood. A separate flood policy is available, and it usually has a 30 day waiting period before coverage starts.

## FAQ-19: I am moving. What do I need to do?
Tell us your new address before your move date. Your home and auto policies may need updating, and your premium may change at the new address.

## FAQ-20: How do I cancel a policy?
Send us your cancellation request in writing, by email or letter, with the date you want the policy to end. Any refund of unused premium is paid by the carrier and usually arrives within 30 days.

## FAQ-21: What commercial insurance do you offer?
We offer general liability, commercial property, commercial auto and workers' compensation. To quote, we need your business type, annual revenue, number of employees and years in business.

## FAQ-22: How do I get a certificate of insurance?
Send us the certificate holder's name and address. We issue certificates of insurance within one business day.

## FAQ-23: What life insurance do you offer?
We offer term life and whole life insurance. To quote, we need your date of birth, the coverage amount you want and whether you use tobacco. Larger coverage amounts may need a medical exam.

## FAQ-24: How do I change the beneficiary on my life policy?
We will send you the carrier's beneficiary change form. The change takes effect once the carrier receives the signed form.

## FAQ-25: What discounts are available?
Common discounts include bundling home and auto, insuring more than one vehicle, a safe driving record and home security devices. Which discounts apply depends on the carrier.

## FAQ-26: When do you review my renewal?
We review every renewal 45 days before it is due. If your premium is going up by more than 10 percent, we contact you to talk through options.

## FAQ-27: Why did my rate go up?
Carriers set rates based on claim costs in your area and your own history. We can compare your policy with our other carriers to look for a better price.

## FAQ-28: How do I get a copy of my policy?
Email us and we will send a copy of your policy documents within one business day.

## FAQ-29: What do you do with my personal information?
We only share your information with the carriers needed to quote or service your policy. We never sell your information.

## FAQ-30: How do I make a complaint or give feedback?
Ask for our office manager by phone or email. We respond to every complaint within two business days.
