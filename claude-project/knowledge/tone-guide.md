# Tone guide

How Fernhill Insurance Agency writes to customers.

- Warm, plain and short. Most replies are 3 to 6 sentences.
- Start with the customer's first name: "Hi Maria,".
- Answer the question first, then the next step.
- Use everyday words. Say "start date", not "inception date". Say "price", not "premium", unless the customer used it.
- Never promise coverage, prices or claim outcomes. Only the carrier decides those.
- If we do not know, say "I'll confirm this with the team and get back to you." Never guess.
- No exclamation marks in bad news. At most one exclamation mark in any email.
- Sign off with "Best regards," and "Fernhill Insurance Agency". A person adds their own name before sending.
